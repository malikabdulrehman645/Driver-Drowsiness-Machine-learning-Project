# Research plan

**Question:** Does context-aware prioritization reduce unnecessary alerts while maintaining safety-critical hazard recall compared with fixed-distance alerts?

**Hypothesis:** CAPME reduces noncritical alerts without a clinically or operationally unacceptable reduction in critical-hazard recall. Define acceptance criteria with supervisor before experiments.

**Controls:** Same sensor-event replay, same labeled scenarios, matched conditions. Test camera-only, cane-only and fused input separately.

**Reporting:** Confusion matrices, per-class precision/recall, alert rate, latency distribution (median/p95), ablations, and limitations. Avoid claims of validated safety without appropriate evidence.
